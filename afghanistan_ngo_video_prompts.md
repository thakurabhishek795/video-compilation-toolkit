# Afghanistan NGO Video Prompts & Storyboard Guide

This guide is inspired by the cinematic, warm, and empowering style of the **Naari Shakti** video. It is designed to help you create a compelling video highlighting an NGO's work with girls and women in Afghanistan.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Style Guide
To match the feel of the *Naari Shakti* video, aim for a **dignified, authentic, and hopeful** tone rather than focusing solely on hardship.
*   **Color Palette:** Warm earth tones (clay, brick, terracotta), deep lapis lazuli blues, crimson/garnet red (from traditional Afghan embroidery), and soft window light.
*   **Camera Style:** Documentary realism. Use natural lighting, shallow depth of field (blurry backgrounds to focus on faces), slow-motion details, and respectful eye-level portraiture.
*   **Vibe:** Empowering, focusing on hands-on craft, learning, sisterhood, and resilience.

---

## 🎬 Storyboard & AI Video Generation Prompts

Here are the step-by-step prompts you can copy and paste into advanced AI video generators (like **Runway Gen-3 Alpha**, **Luma Dream Machine**, **Kling AI**, or **OpenAI Sora**).

````carousel
### Scene 1: Establishing the Setting
**Visual:** Establishing the atmosphere of Afghanistan (e.g., Kabul or a rural village) to anchor the story.

**Runway Gen-3 / Sora Prompt:**
```text
Cinematic documentary establishing shot of a traditional neighborhood in Kabul, Afghanistan. Flat-roof brick and clay houses built along a hillside under a soft golden morning sun. A gentle breeze blowing dust motes through warm sunbeams. Photorealistic, Arri Alexa 35mm lens, natural colors, slow camera pan, high-end film look.
```
<!-- slide -->
### Scene 2: The Safe Space (Gathering)
**Visual:** Showing the transition into the NGO’s center—a safe haven for learning or crafting.

**Runway Gen-3 / Sora Prompt:**
```text
Inside a brightly lit classroom in Afghanistan. A group of young Afghan girls and women sitting in a circle on traditional carpets, studying and writing in notebooks together. Soft natural light streaming through a large window. They are smiling and talking, creating a warm and supportive community atmosphere. Documentary style, authentic, 4k.
```
<!-- slide -->
### Scene 3: Detail of Craft & Empowerment
**Visual:** Close-up shots of hands creating things (sewing, embroidery, coding, or drawing) to emphasize agency and skill-building.

**Runway Gen-3 / Sora Prompt:**
```text
Extreme close-up macro shot of an Afghan woman's hands carefully stitching intricate red, green, and gold patterns onto a dark blue traditional fabric. Shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the needle passing through the cloth. Realistic hand movements, natural skin textures, warm color grading.
```
<!-- slide -->
### Scene 4: Portraits of Dignity & Hope
**Visual:** Individual medium close-ups of the women/girls looking into the camera with strength and pride.

**Runway Gen-3 / Sora Prompt:**
```text
A close-up portrait of a young Afghan woman, around 18 years old, looking directly at the camera with a gentle, confident smile. She is wearing a simple headscarf, and soft window light highlights her features. The background is a softly blurred courtyard. Strong, dignified, hopeful expression. Cinematic lighting, photorealistic.
```
<!-- slide -->
### Scene 5: Unity & Solidarity
**Visual:** The group standing or sitting together, showing mutual support and collective strength.

**Runway Gen-3 / Sora Prompt:**
```text
A group of Afghan women and girls standing together in a sun-drenched courtyard, holding hands in a circle and looking up, smiling with a sense of collective empowerment and solidarity. Slow motion, warm cinematic tones, uplifting and emotional ending.
```
````

## 📝 Video Script & Narrative Structure

If you are writing a script or generating a voiceover (using AI voice tools like ElevenLabs), here is a structure modeled after the *Naari Shakti* flow:

### 1. The Challenge (0:00 - 0:15)
*   **Visuals:** Scene 1 (Establishing neighborhood) transitioning to women walking quietly in the street.
*   **Voiceover Concept:** *"Every girl has a right to learn, to work, and to dream. But for many women and girls in Afghanistan, the paths to these dreams have become narrow, restricted to the walls of their homes."*

### 2. The Haven/Center (0:15 - 0:40)
*   **Visuals:** Scene 2 (Safe space classroom) and Scene 3 (Hands embroidering/studying).
*   **Voiceover Concept:** *"This center is a space where those paths open up again. Here, they gather not just to learn a trade, but to find their voices, support one another, and build a future with their own hands."*

### 3. The Impact (0:40 - 1:10)
*   **Visuals:** Scene 4 (Dignified portraits) and close-ups of finished products or writing.
*   **Voiceover Concept:** *"When you empower a girl, you change a family. When you empower a woman, you rebuild a community. With every stitch, every word written, and every skill gained, they are reclaiming their independence."*

### 4. Call to Action (1:10 - End)
*   **Visuals:** Scene 5 (Group solidarity) transitioning to a logo screen with contact info.
*   **Voiceover Concept:** *"They are not giving up, and neither should we. Join us in supporting Afghan women and girls. Be part of their journey toward hope."*
